Pop Universe · Privacy Policy

How Pop Universe handles your data

Effective 27 Sep 2026 com.zazstudios.popuniverse Published by AmazeIT

Pop Universe is a bubble‑popping puzzle game. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of Pop Universe.

01

Information we collect

Pop Universe does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to play. The app does collect the following, automatically, to keep the game running and to understand how it's used:

  • Anonymous player ID. On first launch the app creates an anonymous sign‑in (via Firebase Authentication) with no personal details attached, used only to save your progress and leaderboard scores.
  • Gameplay progress. Level completion, high scores, and coin balances are stored against that anonymous ID (via Cloud Firestore) so your progress can sync if you reinstall.
  • Usage analytics. Anonymous, aggregated events — such as which game mode was opened or a level was completed — via Firebase Analytics, to help us understand which features are used.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, technical details (via Firebase Crashlytics) are sent to help us fix bugs.
  • Device and app information. Device model, operating system version, and the installed app version, used for compatibility and support.

Game settings such as sound and vibration preferences are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

02

Advertising

Pop Universe shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) to support free play. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to serve ads and measure their performance. On iOS, the app asks for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any data is used for tracking or personalized advertising — you can decline this and still play the game with non‑personalized ads.

Google's own privacy policy covers how AdMob handles this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

03

In‑app purchases

Optional in‑app purchases (such as coin packs) are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Pop Universe never sees or stores your card number, billing address, or other payment details — that information stays with Google or Apple under their own payment and privacy terms.

04

Children's privacy

Pop Universe is a general‑audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information — such as a name, email address, or exact location — from anyone, including children. The anonymous identifiers and gameplay data described above are not personally identifiable on their own.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information that concerns you, contact us at the address below and we will review and delete it.

05

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with the service providers that help operate the app, each bound by their own privacy terms:

ProviderPurpose
Google FirebaseAnonymous sign‑in, save data, analytics, crash reporting
Google AdMobIn‑app advertising
Google Play / Apple App StorePurchases and billing
06

Your choices and data retention

Anonymous gameplay data is kept for as long as your account is active so your progress and leaderboard position are preserved. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing the address below with your in‑game username or approximate last‑played date so we can locate your record. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored settings from your device immediately.

07

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including the security provided by Google Firebase's infrastructure — to protect the data described above. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

08

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back occasionally, especially before providing any information through the app.
